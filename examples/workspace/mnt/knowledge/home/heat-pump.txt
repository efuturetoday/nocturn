Heat pump Thermia Calibra 8 — quick reference

Modes
Three modes: Automatic, Hot water only, and Holiday. Holiday holds the flow temperature at 12 C and
heats water once a week so the pipes do not stagnate.

Heat curve
Factory setting is 1.2. A well-insulated house usually runs at 0.8. Dropping it by 0.1 saves roughly
three percent of electricity, but only shows below five degrees outside.

Error codes
E04 is insufficient flow in the heating circuit. Usually a clogged filter, or too many room
controllers closed at once.
E11 is pressure below 0.8 bar. Refill through the red valve in the plant room, target 1.5 bar cold.
E27 is a communication fault to the outdoor unit and almost always clears after a restart.

Service
Clean the return filter once a year, best in autumn before the heating season. The refrigerant is
maintenance-free and may only be touched by a certified firm.
