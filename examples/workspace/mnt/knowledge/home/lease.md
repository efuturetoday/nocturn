# Lease — 42 Sonnenallee

## Parties and property

Landlord is Nordlicht Property Management. The unit is a three-room flat on the third floor,
78 square metres, west-facing balcony, cellar compartment 14.

## Rent and service charges

Base rent is 1,240 EUR per month, due by the third working day. The service-charge advance is
210 EUR. Operating costs are settled annually on 31 March.

## Deposit

The deposit is three months' base rent — 3,720 EUR — paid in three equal instalments, the last on
1 September. It sits in an insolvency-protected escrow account and is released at the latest six
months after the flat is handed back, less any justified claims.

## Notice

Three months to the end of a month, for either side. Notice must be in writing; email explicitly
does not count.

## Decorating on move-out

Decorating is owed on move-out only if the flat was handed over freshly decorated. It was not, and
the handover record of 14 August says so.
