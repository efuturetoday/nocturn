## What makes it different

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="You're always in the loop" icon="approve-check">
		Looking things up is free, but sending, changing, or deleting always waits for
		your yes. Nothing irreversible happens behind your back.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Approve from your phone" icon="comment">
		Get a push notification, tap Approve or Deny. Because the decision lives on a
		second device, a hijacked chat can't approve its own actions.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Runs on your machine" icon="laptop">
		One program. No cloud account, no database, nothing to install alongside it. Your
		data stays in a folder you own.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Extensible, safely" icon="puzzle">
		Connect it to email, calendars, and APIs with plugins that run locked down and
		still have to ask before they act.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
