A sandwich: light leaves only through the seam around the rim, the lid stays dark. Every point of that band is the additive sum of all seven LEDs, per channel, with 1−e−x tone mapping — red next to green really does turn yellow in between. lid = 0 makes the device fully opaque upwards.
